# LYRIC Standalone Pricing — Final Recommendation

## Correction from earlier analysis
The $497/$797/$1,497 tiers shown are Aims AI's **existing** lead-recovery product — not a competitor. Lyric is a different animal: an AI content studio (create/schedule/publish across channels), not a lead-capture/CRM tool. So Lyric's real competitive set is content/social tools, and it should be priced **below** the existing Aims AI tiers so it doesn't cannibalize them — likely a top-of-funnel or complementary product.

## 1. Who Lyric actually competes with, and what they charge

| Competitor | Price/mo | What they do | What they lack vs Lyric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buffer | $6–$120 | Scheduling, light analytics | No real AI content generation, no email/newsletter, no industry templates |
| Later | $25–$130 | Visual scheduling, Instagram-first | Weak AI, single-platform bias |
| Hootsuite | $99–$249+ (team/enterprise higher) | Enterprise scheduling + reporting | Expensive, clunky, bolt-on AI |
| Sprout Social | $199–$399+ | Enterprise social + engagement | Enterprise-only, no content creation studio |
| Canva (+ Content Planner) | $0–$15 | Design + light scheduling | No AI copywriting, no CRM/lead angle, no industry playbooks |
| ContentStudio / Vista Social | $25–$99 | Multi-platform scheduling + AI captions | Generic AI, no industry-specific playbooks |

**Takeaway:** the category tops out around $200–$400/month even at the "enterprise" end (Sprout, Hootsuite team plans). Nobody in this space is charging $497+ — that price point belongs to lead-gen/CRM tools like our existing product, not content studios.

## 2. Recommended Lyric standalone pricing

Position Lyric **above generic schedulers, below our own lead-recovery product** — priced for the AI + industry-playbook advantage, not for lead generation (it doesn't do that; Allegra/our CRM product does).

| Tier | Price/mo | Positioning |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | **$97/mo** | Solo operators, 1 industry playbook, core content types, basic scheduling |
| **Pro** | **$247/mo** ⭐ | Most customers — multiple platforms, all content types, team seats, analytics |
| **Studio/Agency** | **$497/mo** | Agencies/multi-brand — unlimited seats, white-label, API, priority support |

Why these numbers:
- **$97** undercuts Hootsuite/Sprout while beating Buffer/Later on capability (AI generation + industry templates) — easy "no-brainer" entry price.
- **$247** sits mid-market, above Later/Buffer, below Sprout/Hootsuite enterprise, justified by AI + industry playbooks.
- **$497** caps the standalone line exactly at our existing Starter tier price — deliberate: Lyric never outprices our core product, it's a lighter, adjacent purchase (and a natural upsell path *into* the $497+ tiers once someone's hooked on the content side).

Annual discount: ~15–17% off (standard SaaS convention), e.g. $97→$970/yr, $247→$2,470/yr, $497→$4,970/yr.

## 3. Realistic client acquisition per month (using Allegra internally)

Allegra here = our own prospecting/outreach engine, used to build the list and run outbound for Lyric — not sold to the customer. That's actually the *easier* case: no need to convince someone to buy a second product, just to book a demo.

### Funnel assumptions (content-tool buyer, not enterprise lead-gen buyer — faster sales cycle, lower ticket, more self-serve)
- List quality/reachable: ~70%
- Open/response rate: 20–25%
- Meeting booked (of responses): 20%
- Demo → trial: 60%
- Trial → paid: 20–25% (self-serve-ish product, lower touch than the $497+ line)

### Month-by-month, realistic ramp (not the "unarbitrary 100")

| Period | Outreach volume/mo | New paid customers/mo |
|---|---|---|
| Month 1–2 (pilot, list-building, messaging tests) | 2,000–3,000 | **8–15** |
| Month 3–4 (sequences tuned) | 5,000–8,000 | **20–35** |
| Month 5–6 (playbook working) | 10,000–15,000 | **40–60** |
| Month 7–12 (steady state) | 15,000–20,000 | **60–90** |

**Realistic number to plan around: 40–60 new Lyric customers/month by mid-year, scaling toward 75–90/month by year-end**, assuming one dedicated person running Allegra sequences plus you closing demos. 100/month is reachable but treat it as the stretch case for Q4, not the month-1 baseline — nobody hits full outbound velocity before the list, messaging, and demo-to-close motion are tuned (that tuning is what months 1–4 are for).

### Revenue at the realistic pace (blended ~$200/mo/customer across the 3 tiers)
- Month 6 run rate: ~50 new/mo × $200 blended ≈ $10K new MRR/month, cumulative MRR by month 6 ≈ $35–45K
- Month 12 run rate: ~75 new/mo × $200 ≈ $15K new MRR/month, cumulative MRR by month 12 ≈ $130–160K
- **Year 1 total customers: ~500–600, Year 1 exit MRR: ~$130–160K ($1.6–1.9M ARR run rate)**

### Add-on pricing (Lyric bundled into existing Starter/Pro/Elite customers)

This is a different pricing question than standalone: an add-on has **zero incremental CAC** (no new demo, no new deal — it's an upsell email/in-app prompt to someone already paying us). Standard SaaS practice is to price add-ons at roughly **45–55% of standalone-equivalent value** to reward the existing relationship and maximize attach rate, then match the add-on tier to the sophistication of the base plan it's attached to:

| Existing base tier | Add Lyric capability level | Add-on price/mo | New bundled total/mo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter ($497) | Lyric Starter | **+$79** | $576 |
| Pro ($797) | Lyric Pro | **+$147** | $944 |
| Elite ($1,497) | Lyric Studio/Agency | **+$347** | $1,844 |

Why tiered instead of one flat add-on price: a flat price (e.g. "$197 for everyone") is simpler to sell but underprices the Elite segment, who get the full agency/white-label feature set and can clearly afford more — and it overprices Starter customers relative to what they're using. Tiering the add-on to match the base plan captures more expansion revenue from Elite without creating friction at the low end.

**Expansion revenue impact:** if even 25–30% of an existing base attaches Lyric at these prices, that's pure margin on top of the core product with no new customer acquisition cost — this is usually the highest-ROI lever available (cheaper than any new-customer channel, including Allegra outbound).
