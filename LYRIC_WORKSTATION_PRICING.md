# LYRIC Workstation Pricing Strategy

## Product Overview
LYRIC Workstation is an AI-powered content studio enabling users to create, schedule, and publish content across multiple social platforms and content types with integrated analytics.

**Key Features:**
- Multi-platform distribution (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn)
- Multiple content types (Posts, Reels, Scripts, Carousels, Emails, Newsletters, Blog Posts)
- AI-powered content generation
- Calendar & scheduling
- Publishing management
- Analytics & performance tracking
- Industry-specific templates

---

## Pricing Model Recommendation: Tiered SaaS Subscription

### Rationale
- **Recurring Revenue:** Predictable, scalable revenue model
- **Usage-based Tiers:** Aligns pricing with customer value and growth
- **Clear Value Proposition:** Different tiers serve different customer segments
- **Industry Standard:** Matches successful competitors (Buffer, Hootsuite, Later)

---

## Proposed Pricing Tiers

### 1. STARTER - $29/month (or $290/year - 17% discount)
**For:** Solo content creators, small businesses, new users

**Included:**
- 3 connected social accounts
- Up to 20 posts/month (across all platforms)
- Basic analytics (impressions, engagement, followers)
- Email & Newsletter content types
- Basic AI content generation (50 AI credits/month)
- Single user account
- Standard support (email, 24-48hr response)
- Mobile app access

**Limitations:**
- No team collaboration
- Limited to 3 content types
- Basic scheduling (up to 30 days out)

---

### 2. PROFESSIONAL - $79/month (or $790/year - 17% discount)
**For:** Growing teams, content agencies, active creators

**Included:**
- 10 connected social accounts (unlimited content types)
- Up to 100 posts/month
- Advanced analytics (engagement rates, audience insights, ROI tracking)
- Full content type support (Posts, Reels, Scripts, Carousels, Emails, Newsletters, Blog Posts)
- Enhanced AI generation (200 AI credits/month)
- Up to 5 team members
- Team collaboration & permissions management
- Advanced scheduling (up to 90 days out)
- Bulk scheduling & batch uploads
- Content calendar collaboration
- Priority support (email, chat, 4hr response)
- Monthly performance reports
- Mobile app + desktop access

**Ideal for:**
- Marketing teams (3-5 people)
- Social media agencies (managing 5-10 clients)
- Active content creators monetizing their presence

---

### 3. ENTERPRISE - $299/month (or $2,990/year - 17% discount)
**For:** Large teams, multi-brand management, agencies at scale

**Included:**
- Unlimited connected social accounts
- Unlimited posts/month
- Custom analytics dashboards
- All content types + custom content types
- Premium AI generation (1,000 AI credits/month)
- Unlimited team members
- Advanced team management & role-based permissions
- Custom branding on reports
- Unlimited scheduling horizon
- Advanced integrations (Zapier, webhooks, CRM connectors)
- API access (for custom integrations)
- White-label options available
- Dedicated account manager
- Priority support (phone, chat, 1hr response)
- Quarterly business reviews
- Custom training & onboarding
- Service level agreement (SLA)

**Ideal for:**
- Agencies managing 20+ accounts
- Enterprise brands managing multiple regions/brands
- Organizations requiring custom integrations
