### Three Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)

**ICP #1: Marketing Manager at SMB**
- Company size: 50-500 employees
- Role: VP/Director of Marketing, Social Media Manager
- Pain: Managing 5+ social channels is eating 20+ hours/week
- Budget: $500-2K/year for tools
- Fit: Professional tier ($79/month) + add-ons
- Allegra target: LinkedIn filter by title + company size + industry

**ICP #2: Agency Owner/Digital Marketer**
- Business: 10-50 person digital/content agency
- Pain: Need better tools to manage multiple client accounts
- Budget: $1-5K/month for agency tools
- Fit: Professional-Enterprise tiers with white-label add-on
- Allegra target: Agency titles, industry codes (62XX in NAICS)

**ICP #3: Freelance Creator/Influencer**
- Following: 10K-500K across social platforms
- Income: $1K-10K/month through content
- Pain: Too much time scheduling, wants to create more
- Budget: $30-100/month
- Fit: Starter-Professional tiers
- Allegra target: Creators (LinkedIn "content creator," "freelancer" + follower count proxies)

## Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Allegra overreach fatigue | Low conversion | Segment campaigns, personalize heavily, monitor engagement |
| Can't reach 100/mo | Revenue miss | Shift to agency partnerships (higher LTV, lower volume) |
| High early churn | Revenue instability | Invest in onboarding, customer success playbook |
| Competitor price cuts | Margin pressure | Focus on AI differentiation (hard to replicate quickly) |
| Sales team turnover | Execution risk | Invest in culture, clear comp plan, proven playbook |
