## Implementation: Premium Path (Recommended)

### Month 1-2: Foundation
- **Industry Specialization:**
  - Pick 2-3 verticals: Roofing, HVAC, Real Estate (highest ROI industries)
  - Build industry-specific templates, workflows, lead scoring
  - Gather case studies from beta customers
  
- **Sales Playbook:**
  - Create 3 different pitch decks (one per vertical)
  - Build ROI calculator (like competitor shows industry-specific ROI)
  - Develop industry-specific case studies
  
- **Allegra Setup:**
  - Target service business owners by SIC code
  - Segment by location count (1 location, 2-5, 5+)
  - Messaging focused on "lead recovery" + "content automation"

- **Target:** 15-20 customers

### Month 3-4: Refinement
- **Sales Velocity:**
  - Analyze which vertical closes fastest (probably Roofing or Real Estate based on competitor benchmarks)
  - Double down on winning vertical
  - Gather customer testimonials with ROI numbers
  
- **Product Optimization:**
  - Add industry-specific integrations (Salesforce, dispatch software, accounting)
  - Build pre-built campaigns for top vertical
  
- **Target:** 30-40 customers

### Month 5-6: Scale
- **Content Marketing:**
  - Industry publications (Roofing Magazine, HVAC Weekly, etc.)
  - LinkedIn thought leadership (how service businesses use AI)
  - Case studies with ROI numbers ($8K-25K ROI like competitor shows)
  
- **Partnership Development:**
  - Software integrations (QuickBooks, Square, Stripe for service businesses)
  - Partner channels (agencies serving service businesses)

- **Target:** 50-70 customers

### Month 7-12: Acceleration
- **Paid Advertising:**
  - LinkedIn ads to service business owners
